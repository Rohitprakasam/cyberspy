05 — PRIVACY & SECURITY BY DESIGN
🔒
Data Never Leaves Without Consent
All monitoring data is stored exclusively on the victim's device. Nothing is transmitted to CyberSpy servers until the user explicitly presses the Report button.
🔑
Hardware-Backed Encryption
All shards are encrypted using AES-256-GCM with keys stored in Android Keystore (hardware security module). Keys cannot be extracted even with root access.
⚖️
Court-Admissible Evidence
Every evidence package includes SHA-256 integrity hashes and PKI digital signatures, meeting requirements of Indian Evidence Act Section 65B for electronic records.
🛡️
DPDP Act 2023 Compliant
Full compliance with India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act 2023, GDPR-aligned for global markets. Explicit consent, right to delete, no data monetization.
